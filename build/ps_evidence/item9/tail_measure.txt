Sampling once a tick something that now moves per drawn frame aliases: ticks land at uneven points
between 144 Hz frames, so --old and --built read high here while they are smooth per drawn frame (above,
both 0.135). The PT11 "0.45 -> 0.31" per-tick win was largely this artifact; per drawn frame PT11 made
the tail step (0.135 -> 1.993).

Reading: a smooth sway sampled at 144 Hz has jerk/motion of about 0.1 (its own curvature); 0.135 is the
tail's own wag. The 60 Hz step was 2.0 (PT11). Before PT11 the tail was already smooth in her own frame,
but the camera and so the whole world stepped at 60 Hz (world jerk 31 m over 15.6 m of motion); now the
world moves evenly too (0.0016 m), her body stays put on screen (0.0002 m).
