# Independent Fable AAA review, round 1 (2026-11-09)

An independent Fable 5 reviewer saw 70 fresh headless renders (world at four times of day, screens, casino, letters, title, two cinematics) with only the owner's playtest words and the reference games. It was not shown code, notes or claims.

## Verdict: NOT YET

Scores: world art and lighting 5 (buildings 7, backdrop/ground/night/rain 3); characters 5; UI and HUD 4; menus and screens 5 (casino tables 7; letters, depot, creator, title 3); cinematics 3; cohesion 5.

## The ten problems, ranked (abridged)

1. Night is not night: flat navy sky with no moon or stars, hills and grass stay lit, no emissive windows or lamp halos.
2. Empty backdrop: three untextured lime domes; no treeline, no Canterlot, no daytime clouds; one giant cloud blob over the plaza.
3. The HUD is six text rectangles: a "Getting started" box, a compass bar, an objective banner, a saddlebag box, a persistent scoop hint and an "H controls" chip; the toast stacks on the hint.
4. Pens have no presence: thin posts, one rail the colour of the grass, no gate, trough or straw; a 6 px price plaque.
5. Camera and follower occlusion: Spike, followers and NPCs fill or clip the frame; no near-lens fade.
6. Ground and shading: a flat path with a blurry edge, soft blob shadows, the toon terminator splitting faces, a grey sphere at a pony's mouth.
7. Rain is screen streaks; it is missing entirely from houses_nightrain.
8. Menus are paragraphs in boxes: Depot empty state, rules lists, word-processor letters, a web-form creator, market copy truncated.
9. Title and loading: no logotype, a "> New game" caret, two titles on the loading screen.
10. Cinematics don't frame their subject: Derpy and Rainbow are 20 to 30 px; the boom ring has no flash, shake or bloom.

Lower: floating "!" bubbles; blackjack face cards are letter rectangles; slot reels show no blur.

## Keep

The architecture; the plaza composition; dusk grading; the casino hall and tables (the house style for every menu); the race form guide; the catch card's content; the sign language; letterbox framing and the letters' copy; Spike's face.
