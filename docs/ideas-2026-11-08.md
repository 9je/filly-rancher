# Ideas to try, 2026-11-08

From the 2026-11-08 playtest and the references talk. Steer by Viva Pinata (the loop),
Cult of the Lamb (tone and story beats) and Pokemon Legends: Arceus (catching). Keep Slime
Rancher for look and feel only. Each idea is an experiment: build the smallest version, the
owner plays it, then keep, tweak or cut.

## A. Catching: skill, not hit counts (Legends: Arceus)
A1. One clean catch again: the beam lifts every pony on the first hold.
A2. Difficulty from behaviour: skittish ponies bolt if they see you, so you sneak up from behind at a walk. That gives walking its job (playtest 25).
A3. Bait: drop a pony's favourite food, she comes and eats, and while she eats she is easy to catch.
A4. A Pony Log page per kind: seen, caught, fed her favourite, Bloomed, raced. Filling rows pays out.

## B. Ponies who earn their keep (Palworld)
B1. Bloomed ponies work at their talent and you see them doing it: earth ponies tend a food patch that fills the pantry, pegasi move clouds or water the patch, unicorns polish gems in the tray.
B2. Better cared for means worth more: a happy, Bloomed pony makes more and is worth more at the Depot. Caring for her makes her more valuable to the Factory, which is the horror.

## C. Who shows up, and when (Viva Pinata, Animal Crossing)
C1. Rare ponies are gated by time, weather and what you have built: Luna only at night, Rainbow Dash after a storm, Fluttershy once a flower bed is down. At most one new legendary a day.
C2. Visit cards: a pony's card shows what makes her visit, stay and Bloom, as checkboxes that tick as you meet them.
C3. Ranch decorations that matter: a flower bed, an apple tree, a cloud perch. Each pulls in kinds and speeds up Bloom.

## D. Pace and money (Stardew)
D1. Real days: sleep at the house to end the day and save, which fixes autosave (playtest 3) and lets you skip to dawn (playtest 11).
D2. Replace the hidden 5,000 floor with a visible weekly quota on collection day, with requests by kind ("Cloudsdale wants 2 pegasi this week").
D3. Saturation: the more of one kind you deliver in a week, the less each one pays, so variety becomes the strategy.
D4. A ladder of things to buy: saddlebag, beam range and strength, pens, decorations and gadgets, so there is always a next purchase.

## E. Tone and story beats (Cult of the Lamb)
E1. Collection day as a staged scene: the Collectors arrive and you pick who goes from your pens, and it lands on camera instead of in a letter.
E2. Named ponies remember you: a line when you feed them, a reaction when you pen them, so delivering one hurts.
E3. Event cinematics: the Bloom ceremony, the Sonic Rainboom, a Derpy crash-landing, a race finish. Short camera cuts that also show off the jiggle.

## Order to try (after playtest blockers 1 to 3)
1. A1 to A3, catching. Small, and it fixes the most-felt annoyance.
2. D1 and C1, days with sleep, and rares gated by time and weather. These fix "everything in 65 minutes".
3. E1 with D2, a staged collection day with a visible quota. This replaces the floor that confused everyone.
4. B1 and B2, jobs and value, which give keeping ponies a point.
5. C2, C3 and A4, the visit cards, decorations and the Pony Log. The long tail of collecting.
6. E2, E3, D3 and D4, the polish and depth layers.

## F. Cutscenes and cinematics (owner: "cutscenes and cinematics wanted")
Rules: short (5 to 20 s for events, up to about 60 s for story), letterboxed, can always be
skipped, never ambushes you mid-menu (it waits until you are free). Every story crack gets
emphasis through the camera, sound and timing, with innocent words (see memory:
story-cracks-subtle).

Story:
F1. Arrival: the Cloudsdale balloon drops you at the ranch, Marigold waves, the camera passes over the valley.
F2. First catch, first pen: a small celebratory beat.
F3. First Bloom: the ceremony, with the camera pushing in to her flank as the mark appears. Every later Bloom gets a short version, and you are called over when you are away.
F4. Collection day: the Collectors' cart arrives, you choose who goes, they are led away, the camera stays on the empty pen a second too long.
F5. Debt cleared: Marigold's smile holds, and "bits won't do for the rest, dear".
F6. Color Day: the dawn, the lift, the ride up to Cloudsdale.
F7. The reveal inside the Factory, the path choice, the climax lever, and the epilogue for each of the 5 endings.

World events:
F8. Sonic Rainboom across the sky.
F9. Derpy crash-landing (tumble, bounce, flank jiggle).
F10. Race finish: the photo finish, your pick highlighted, a win or loss reaction.
F11. Legendary sighting: a 3 s reveal of where she is, then the trail.
F12. A big casino win: the jackpot camera and the crowd reacts.

Tech: one shared cinematic player (camera rails, letterbox, skip, input lock, a hold that
waits for menus to close), so each scene is data, not new code.

## G. The colour loop (chosen 2026-11-08, replaces B1 for now)
The Factory only wants colour. A blank Drifter fetches a token intake fee and does not count
toward the quota; a Bloomed pony counts and pays. Bloom is fast (3 to 4 min under good care)
and readable (a meter and one blocking reason per pony), so caring is the loop between
catching trips, and caring well is exactly what makes her worth more to the Factory. B2 is
folded in; B1 (jobs) waits. Full detail: docs/sprint-plan-2026-11-08.md section 2.1.
