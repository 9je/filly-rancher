# Fable AAA review, round 2 (2026-11-09, fresh reviewer, 87 images in scratchpad showcase2)

Verdict: NOT YET. Scores: world 5, characters 4, UI and HUD 6, menus 6, cinematics 3, cohesion 5.
(Round 1: 5, 5, 4, 5, 3, 5.)

Ranked problems:
1. Sun and moon drawn with a dashed black ellipse outline (hill_*, derpy_0, rainboom_1..4).
2. Ground a flat single colour with identical tufts every ~1.5 m; no breakup, no clover/dirt patches.
3. Ponies smooth-shaded, not cel; manes one mass; Derpy lower tier, no outline. The catch card art is the target.
4. Near-camera dither reads as a checkerboard at native pixel size (player_flank, pens_*, catch_settled).
5. Pens are translucent glass boxes; Pens sign flat; price signs illegible.
6. Cinematics: Derpy's head cropped (derpy_4/5); Rainboom ring reads as a 2D overlay, captions plain text.
7. Night/dusk/rain: saturated grass at night, dusk a sepia filter, no wet ground, rainbow at night.
8. Market and Depot are web forms: grey glyph icons, olive Buy slab, tiny white Back pill everywhere.
9. HUD noise: permanent Scoop prompt and Getting started; huge "!" marker at the screen edge; unreadable compass; 0/0 and 0/12 counters overlap.
10. Catch card: portrait crop off-centre, stars twice, weak sunburst, HUD visible behind.

Also: menus open during cinematics (market_buy_top); creator option thumbnails not the player's pony, empty right panel, stray gold half-ring; casino games float on a void; Requests board black; mountains translucent; Pens sign peeking behind Quit on the title.

Keep: Ponyville architecture, the flat sky, the casino hall, the plum/gold/cream UI language, the catch card concept and 2D art, the letters, pony court cards, Spike, the writing, the title composition.
