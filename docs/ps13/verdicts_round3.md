## G11
**Verdict:** NOT-MET
**Looked at:** build/ps_evidence/itemG11/README.txt and all 17 logs (the PERF and PERF_FAILED lines of each); game/scenes/test/perf.gd lines 860 to 895 (p99 = sorted[int(n * 0.99)], PERF_OK only when p99 is at or under BUDGET_MS and the worst second holds 60 fps); game/test/test_body_space.gd and game/test/test_rump_bounce.gd (run headless, 16/16 and 9/9); `git diff --stat 3586582 HEAD -- game` (body_space.gd +224/-51, pony.gd, rump_bounce.gd).

Every one of the 17 saved runs prints PERF_FAILED: after the change, p99 39.97 / 41.06 / 43.17 ms at 1920x1080 and 28.35 / 32.50 / 28.87 ms at 960x540, so the row is NOT-MET on its own terms. Is the OPEN description honest? Largely yes. It says "Not met" and "NOT MET: PERF_OK" in plain words; every number quoted in the row (before/after averages, p99s, worst seconds, the section timings, the 31.9 ms run with pony physics off) matches a saved log or the README table; the mechanism changes it describes (neighbour-cell solve, turn checks only within reach, sleeping springs) are pinned by tests that pass, and test_the_solve_gives_what_the_pair_rules_give and test_a_turn_is_allowed_as_the_pair_rules_allow_it do assert the new paths give the old answers, which is what "no pony behaves differently" rests on. Four things the row leaves out or gets loose: (1) README section 1 states the measurements were taken while a separate 1280x720 --fixed-fps 144 render job held the CPU at 100% (load average 4 to 7); the row never says so, and a reader would take its p99s as the machine's idle numbers. (2) The "almost empty scene: p99 13.3 ms" floor and the "EARLIER PASS" runs against 2099bcc^ have no saved log; they are quoted, not evidenced. (3) The row says in one place the invisible display "adds about 20 ms of presentation to every frame" and in another that an empty scene costs 6.8 ms average / 13.3 ms p99 there; both cannot be the floor. (4) "the 15 ms pass recorded in PROGRESS was measured in a real window before the sweep" is unverifiable from the evidence folder. None of these makes the OPEN claim false; they make its attribution of the gap to the display less proven than it reads. To close the row: a run of perf.gd printing PERF_OK, which on the owner's rule (no visible window) means either a measurement on the owner's screen or a saved comparison that isolates the display's cost (the same probe with --window at the owner's resolution against a saved empty-scene run, on an idle machine).
