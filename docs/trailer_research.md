# Trailer research notes (2026-10-23)

Gathered by a research subagent for TRAILER_PLAN.md. Only Valve's doc and two secondary guides were read in full; the per-trailer notes are from memory, not fresh viewing, so treat shot lengths as approximate.

## Rules
- Valve (partner.steamgames.com/doc/store/trailer): the first trailer should be primarily gameplay, with the HUD visible. Viewers may give it under 10 s in the Discovery Queue, and may watch with no audio.
- Store autoplay is muted (per the guides): the trailer must read silently, and the opening should earn an unmute.
- Hook, 0-5 s: the most distinctive moving gameplay shot. Never open on a logo, a fade from black, a title, narration or static art. Shoppers scrub for raw gameplay (Zukowski, via wnhub).
- Length: 60-90 s for the main trailer. The core loop should be readable by about 15 s.
- Shots: 2-4 s, faster in the escalation, varied rhythm.
- Text: about 5 words per card, 3 s or less on screen, few cards, overlaid on gameplay. Only the final card carries much text.
- Music: matches the tone, cuts land on beats, the climax peaks on a hit. Game SFX audible under it.
- End card: title (a store would add a CTA; this build is not for redistribution, so no "Wishlist" line).
- Audio: stereo AAC at 48 kHz, about -14 LUFS integrated, true peak at or below -1 dBTP (web convention, not Valve's).
- Steam builds a 6 s microtrailer from six 1 s clips, so the trailer needs six strong 1 s moments.
- The opening 3-6 s should loop on its own.

## Reference trailers (from memory)
- **Slime Rancher 2:** the vacuum action is in the first second; cuts of 1.5-3 s; the core loop shown in 15 s.
- **Dredge:** a pretty-but-uneasy palette; the loop first, then dread through fog, night and a mutated catch; the music shift marks the turn; the title lands after the strangest image.
- **Cult of the Lamb:** cute and grim in the same frame; cuts of 1-2.5 s.
- **Stardew Valley:** a montage of activities, about 2-3 s each.
- **Strange Horticulture:** restrained, mood-led hints.
- **Unpacking:** the loop is readable in 3 s.
- **Inscryption:** the glitch cut and the held wrong note.
