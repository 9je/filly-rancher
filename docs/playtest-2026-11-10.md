## Blockers and pacing
1. QUIT POINT. Early game dead end: pens full of ponies you want to keep, no bits to expand, objective "Keep Drifters fed to Bloom 0/2", nothing to do but wait. Slime Rancher lets you actively push growth. Needs a player-driven way to speed Bloom, or other productive work [13][24].
2. After the first one-of-a-kinds, the next step is unclear: nothing says to deliver at the Depot, or that gems turn into bits for pen upgrades [13].
3. Idle play wins: leaving the game running during a shower bloomed the Drifters, but every time-limited event fired and expired with nobody there. Needs events that wait for the player, or a "while you were away" recap.
4. After Color Day, "Cut pylons at night" means killing time until dark; the bed only sleeps till dawn, which skips the night. Needs a "wait until night" option.
5. Trixie, Derpy and Rainbow Dash events, plus several notifications, all fired at once. Needs a queue, spacing and priority.
6. A "race day" notice appeared though the owner had raced the day before.
